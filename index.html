QUESTIONS

How fast do we see results?

Engagement rises through the first week. Walk-ins and bookings start climbing in week two. Footfall lift by day 10-15.

Who films and edits the content?

Our vetted local creators handle everything. Filming on-site at your venue, then editing on their unique style. You don't lift a finger beyond letting them in the door.

What if I have no Instagram or TikTok yet?

We set up and optimise your profiles before launch and take care of photography if needed (no extra charge). Our system builds your presence even from zero.

What if I run a small place?

Especially if you run a small place. Local recognition is the entire game. A small spa or café in Belfast gets disproportionately more from being "the place everyone's seeing" than a chain ever does.

What if a creator drops out?

We replace them before the campaign goes live. Your roster of 7-12 is locked in by launch day, no exceptions.

Who owns the content?

All content created through GazeProfit is designed for organic distribution. Videos are published collaboratively across both the creator’s profile and your business profile as part of the campaign rollout. Because creator identity and authenticity are central to performance, content is not licensed for independent reposting, paid advertising, or external commercial use outside the campaign without prior agreement.

What investment should I expect?

Campaigns currently fall between £2,500–£4,500, depending on rollout scale, creator allocation, and campaign objectives. Every campaign is built around your venue, audience, and growth goals.
